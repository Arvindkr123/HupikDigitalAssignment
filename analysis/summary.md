# Search visibility: what to fix first

The export contains 215 cleaned query-and-page combinations. About **38.5% of recorded clicks** came from searches containing Harbourline's name or a spelling variation. The remaining clicks came from searches that did not use the clinic's name; many were local or service-related.

I found **25 quick-win queries**: non-branded searches with at least 100 impressions and an average position from 4 through 20. The score is a rough estimate, not a promise: it compares current click-through rate with a simple position-based benchmark, then gives a modest boost to services and suburbs Harbourline actually serves. 37 queries appeared on more than one page; those should have one clear page to focus on.

## Three things to fix first

1. Make the Chatswood and Parramatta pages stronger local destinations, starting with **physio chatswood**: put the location details, matching services, and call/book action near the top.
2. Improve the relevant landing pages for `physio near me`, `physiotherapist near me`; make the next step to call or book obvious and check that the page matches what the searcher wants.
3. Review the overlapping-page list and choose one best page for each important query; update internal links so the same query is not split across competing pages.
